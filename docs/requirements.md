# Inspire Global Access (IGA)

## Website Requirements Document

---

## About This Document

This document outlines the requirements for the Inspire Global Access website. It describes what the website should do, what pages are needed, and how visitors will interact with it.

---

## 1. Company Overview

|                     |                                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Company Name**    | Inspire Global Access (IGA)          |
| **Website Address** | inspireglobalaccess.com              |
| **Business Type**   | Consulting & Advisory Firm           |
| **Services**        | Education, Employment, Travel, Trade |
| **Target Markets**  | China ↔ Africa (South Africa focus)  |

### What IGA Does

IGA is an **advisory and facilitation firm** that connects individuals and institutions to verified international opportunities.

### Important Note

IGA is **NOT** a recruitment agency or travel agent. This must be clearly communicated on the website.

> **Disclaimer (to appear on website):**  
> "We do not sell jobs, visas, or guarantee placements. All opportunities are verified through partner institutions. Final decisions are made by partners, not IGA."

---

## 2. Brand & Design

### Colors

| Color     | Usage                                |
| --------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Blue**  | Primary/main color                   |
| **White** | Background, clean spaces             |
| **Gold**  | Accents, highlights (used sparingly) |

### Look & Feel

- Professional and clean (benchmark: [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/), [BCG](https://www.bcg.com/))
- Corporate consulting style — advisory, not agency
- Trustworthy and international
- Works well on mobile phones
- Bold headlines, minimal animation, high-quality imagery

### What to Avoid

- Loud or flashy colors
- Excessive animations
- Anything that looks like a "job agency" or "travel agent"

### Brand Tone

> **Global. Ethical. Verified. Strategic. Trustworthy.**

### Logo

A professional consulting-style logo is needed. It will be used on:

- Website
- Email signatures
- Documents and proposals

## 3. Website Pages

### 3.1 Home Page

**Purpose:** First impression — visitors should understand who IGA is within 5 seconds.

**What visitors will see:**

- Strong headline about IGA
- Brief company description
- The 4 main services (with icons)
- Geographic focus (China-Africa)
- Trust statements
- Buttons: "Book a Consultation" / "Explore Opportunities" / "Partner With Us"

---

### 3.2 About Page

**Purpose:** Build trust and credibility.

**What visitors will see:**

- Who IGA is
- Mission and vision
- Company values (Integrity, Transparency, Verification, Accountability)
- Clear statement: "What We Are NOT" (not selling jobs/visas)
- Optional: Team information

---

### 3.3 Services Pages

There will be **one main Services page** and **4 individual service pages**:

#### Service 1: Global Education & Talent Mobility

- **For:** Students, graduates, institutions
- **Includes:** Advisory, eligibility assessments, documentation guidance

#### Service 2: Workforce Access & Employment Pathways

- **For:** Skilled professionals, employers
- **Includes:** Advisory, assessments, partner coordination
- **Disclaimer:** "IGA does not sell jobs or guarantee employment"

#### Service 3: Travel, Exchange & Cultural Programs

- **For:** Travelers, exchange participants
- **Includes:** Structured programs, verified pathways

#### Service 4: Trade, Sourcing & Business Enablement

- **For:** Businesses, importers/exporters
- **Includes:** Trade connections, sourcing support

Each service page will have:

- Who it is for
- What support includes
- A button to request consultation

---

### 3.4 How It Works Page

**Purpose:** Explain the process clearly (protects IGA legally).

**The 5-Step Process:**

1. **Initial Consultation** — Client meets with IGA
2. **Eligibility Assessment** — IGA evaluates readiness
3. **Opportunity Matching** — Find suitable opportunities
4. **Documentation Support** — Help with paperwork
5. **Partner Decision** — Partner institution makes final decision

> **Note:** "Final decisions are made by partner institutions, not IGA."

---

### 3.5 Opportunities Page

**Purpose:** Show available jobs, study programs, and exchange opportunities.

**Features:**

- List of current opportunities
- Filter by category (Education / Employment / Travel / Trade)
- Each listing shows: Title, Category, Location, Partner
- "Apply" or "Learn More" button

**Disclaimer:** "All opportunities are subject to partner approval."

---

### 3.6 Book a Consultation Page

**Purpose:** Allow visitors to book and pay for a consultation.

**Information collected:**

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone/WhatsApp number
- Country
- Consultation type (Education / Employment / Travel / Trade / Partnership)
- Preferred date
- Message (optional)

**Important:**

- Show consultation fee (e.g., "Starting from $XX")
- Payment required before confirmation
- "Consultation fees are non-refundable"
- "No guaranteed outcomes"

---

### 3.7 Partnerships Page

**Purpose:** Attract institutional partners (B2B).

**For:**

- Universities
- Employers
- Institutions
- Trade partners

**Features:**

- Types of partnerships explained
- Benefits of partnering with IGA
- Partnership inquiry form

---

### 3.8 Contact Page

**Contact Information:**

- Email address
- WhatsApp number
- WeChat ID
- Countries of operation

**Contact Form:**

- Name
- Email
- Country
- Subject
- Message

---

### 3.9 Blog

**Purpose:** Share articles, news, and insights.

**Features:**

- List of articles
- Individual article pages
- Categories/tags
- Easy to update (content management)

---

### 3.10 Legal Pages

- **Privacy Policy** — How user data is handled
- **Terms of Service** — Website usage terms
