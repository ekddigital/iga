INSPIRE GLOBAL ACCESS (IGA)
Website Content & Functional Requirements Document
1. WEBSITE PURPOSE & ROLE

The Inspire Global Access (IGA) website is not just an informational site.
It is a trust-building, lead-qualification, and advisory engagement platform designed to:

• Establish credibility with institutions, partners, and clients
• Explain IGA’s ethical advisory model
• Qualify users before engagement
• Convert visitors into consultation bookings
• Protect the company legally and reputationally

2. DESIGN & BRAND DIRECTION
A. Visual Identity

Style:
• Professional
• Clean
• Corporate consulting aesthetic
• International and institutional-friendly

Color Palette:
• Primary: Blue
• Secondary: White
• Accent: Gold (used sparingly for highlights, not dominance)

Avoid:
• Loud colors
• Flashy animations
• Job-selling or travel-agent visuals
• Informal or promotional language

Tone & Brand Personality:
Global. Ethical. Verified. Strategic. Trustworthy.

3. CORE WEBSITE PAGES & FULL CONTENT STRUCTURE
3.1 HOME PAGE – Trust, Clarity & Direction
Purpose:

To immediately communicate who IGA is, what it does, and why it is trustworthy within 5 seconds.

Key Sections:
Hero Section

Headline (example):
“Verified Global Opportunities. Ethical Access. Strategic Partnerships.”

Sub-headline:
Inspire Global Access is a global advisory and facilitation firm connecting individuals and institutions to verified education, employment, travel, and trade opportunities.

Primary Call-to-Actions (CTAs):
• Book a Consultation
• Explore Opportunities
• Partner With Us

About Snapshot

Short introduction explaining:
• Who IGA is
• What advisory role it plays
• Who it serves

Core Practice Areas

Display the four main services clearly:

Global Education & Talent Mobility

Workforce Access & Employment Pathways

Travel, Exchange & Cultural Programs

Trade, Sourcing & Business Enablement

Each with:
• Short description
• “Learn More” link

Geographic Focus

• China – Africa (including South Africa)
• Global institutional partnerships

Trust Signals

• Ethical disclaimers
• “We do not sell jobs or visas”
• “All opportunities are verified through partners”

3.2 ABOUT INSPIRE GLOBAL ACCESS – Credibility Page
Purpose:

To establish authority, ethics, and transparency.

Content Structure:
Who We Are

IGA is a global consulting and advisory firm that facilitates access to verified international opportunities through strategic partnerships.

Our Mission

To provide ethical, transparent, and structured access to global opportunities while protecting clients from misinformation and exploitation.

Our Values

• Integrity
• Transparency
• Verification
• Professional accountability

What We Are NOT

Clear statements such as:
• We do not sell jobs, visas, or guaranteed placements
• We do not act as agents for illegal or unverified processes

3.3 SERVICES / PRACTICE AREAS

Each service should have its own dedicated section or page.

1. Global Education & Talent Mobility

Who it is for:
Students, graduates, and institutions seeking international education pathways.

Support Includes:
• Advisory consultations
• Eligibility assessments
• Documentation guidance
• Partner coordination

CTA: Request Advisory

2. Workforce Access & Employment Pathways

Who it is for:
Skilled professionals, institutions, and employers.

Important Disclaimer:
IGA does not sell jobs or guarantee employment.

CTA: Apply for Assessment

3. Travel, Exchange & Cultural Programs

Focus:
Structured programs, exchanges, and verified travel pathways.

CTA: Request Consultation

4. Trade, Sourcing & Business Enablement

Focus:
Connecting businesses to verified sourcing and trade opportunities.

CTA: Partner With Us

3.4 HOW IT WORKS – Process Transparency Page
Purpose:

This page protects IGA legally and ethically.

Suggested Process Flow:

Initial Consultation

Eligibility & Readiness Assessment

Opportunity Matching

Documentation Support

Partner Review & Decision

Important Note:
Final decisions are made by partner institutions, not IGA.

3.5 BOOK A CONSULTATION – Conversion Page
Required Features:

• Online booking form or calendar
• Consultation category selection:
– Education
– Employment
– Trade
– Partnerships

Display Clearly:

• Advisory fees (even “starting from”)
• No guaranteed outcomes
• Payment before consultation

3.6 OPPORTUNITIES PAGE
Features:

• Listings for job, study, and exchange opportunities
• Filterable categories
• Disclaimer that opportunities are subject to partner approval

3.7 PARTNERSHIPS PAGE – B2B Focus
Target Audience:

• Universities
• Employers
• Institutions
• Trade partners

Include:

• Partnership models
• Institutional inquiry form
• Professional, corporate language

3.8 CONTACT PAGE
Must Include:

• Email
• WhatsApp
• WeChat
• Contact form
• Countries of operation

4. FUNCTIONAL & TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
Mandatory Features:

• Mobile-first design
• Secure forms (SSL)
• Booking & payment system
• Document upload (PDF, images)
• CMS for easy updates
• Professional email integration
• SEO-ready structure
• Fast loading (Africa & Asia optimized)

5. SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

• Secure document storage
• Encrypted forms
• User access control (admin dashboard)
• Clear privacy policy & disclaimers

6. LOGO & BRANDING

• Logo design required
• Clean, professional, consulting-style
• Usable on website, email, and documents

7. BUDGET & IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH

• Phase 1: Affordable MVP launch
• Phase 2: Feature expansion
• Avoid unnecessary complexity
• Focus on credibility and conversion first
